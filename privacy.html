VYRO Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VYRO ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, shares, protects, and deletes information when you use the VYRO mobile application.

VYRO is intended only for users aged 18 and above.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use VYRO, we may collect:

  • Email address and account authentication information
  • User ID and account identifiers
  • Profile name or nickname
  • Date of birth or age
  • Gender, height, city, biography, and interests
  • Profile photos and album photos
  • Chat messages and chat media
  • Likes, matches, favorites, blocks, reports, and profile interactions
  • Location information used for nearby-user and distance features
  • Device, advertising, diagnostic, and app-usage information collected by third-party SDKs where applicable

2. Location Information

VYRO may request access to your device location to provide nearby-user and distance-related features.

Precise latitude and longitude may be processed to calculate distance, but precise coordinates are not intended to be displayed directly to other users.

Third-party advertising or analytics services may derive approximate location from information such as an IP address, subject to their own privacy practices.

3. Profile Information

Information added to your profile may be visible to other VYRO users according to the app's design and your available privacy controls. This may include your profile name, photo, age, city, biography, interests, and other profile details.

4. Photos, Albums, and Media

Users may upload profile photos, album photos, and chat images. VYRO may use automated image moderation services to help detect prohibited, explicit, unsafe, or inappropriate content.

Private or locked album features may restrict access to selected users, but users should only upload content they are legally permitted to share.

5. Messages and Communication

Messages and chat media may be stored to provide messaging functionality. Certain chat media may expire or become unavailable after a limited viewing period.

VYRO may also apply automated or manual safety measures where appropriate to protect users and enforce platform rules.

6. Likes, Matches, Favorites, Blocks, and Reports

VYRO may store interaction data such as profile views, likes, matches, favorites, blocks, reports, and related safety actions to provide social discovery features and support abuse prevention.

7. Authentication

VYRO may use Supabase Authentication and Google Sign-In to create and manage accounts and login sessions. Authentication providers may process account information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Advertising

VYRO may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising technologies may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and other information for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

9. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage, realtime features, and backend services
  • Google Sign-In for authentication
  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Image moderation providers for automated safety checks

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

10. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain VYRO features
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Display profiles and nearby-user recommendations
  • Provide chat, likes, matches, favorites, and album features
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Detect spam, abuse, fraud, prohibited content, or unsafe behavior
  • Respond to reports and enforce platform rules
  • Display and measure advertising where applicable
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

11. Data Sharing

VYRO does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers where necessary to operate the app, provide advertising, perform moderation, maintain security, or comply with legal requirements.

Information users choose to place on their profiles may be visible to other VYRO users as part of the app's social features.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data. Data transmitted between the app and backend services is generally protected using encrypted network connections such as HTTPS/TLS.

No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

13. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain safety, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

Certain chat records and associated chat media may be automatically deleted after a limited retention period where such functionality is enabled.

14. Children's Privacy and Safety

VYRO is intended only for adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly allow children under 18 to create or use VYRO accounts.

VYRO has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), grooming, sextortion involving minors, or any sexualization or exploitation of children.

Users may report other users or inappropriate content through in-app reporting features. VYRO may take action including content removal, account restriction or termination, preservation of relevant information where legally required, and reporting to appropriate authorities as required by applicable law.

See our published VYRO Child Safety Standards.

15. Your Choices and Controls

  • Update profile information
  • Change or remove profile photos
  • Manage album photos and private album access
  • Block or unblock users
  • Report users or content
  • Manage location permission through device settings
  • Permanently delete the VYRO account

16. Account Deletion

VYRO users may permanently delete their account from within the app.

  1. Open VYRO and sign in.
  2. Go to Me / Profile.
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

When an account deletion request is successfully completed, VYRO deletes the account and associated user data where applicable, including profile information and VYRO-hosted user content.

Certain limited information may be retained only when reasonably necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our policies.
