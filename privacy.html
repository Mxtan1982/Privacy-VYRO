Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VYRO ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the VYRO mobile application.

By using VYRO, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide when creating and using your account, including:

  • Email address
  • Profile name or nickname
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • Height
  • City
  • Profile description
  • Interests
  • Profile photos and album photos
  • Messages and chat media
  • Likes, matches, blocks, and reports

2. Location Information

VYRO may request access to your device location to provide nearby-user features and distance information.

Your precise latitude and longitude are not intended to be displayed directly to other users. Location information may be used to calculate approximate distance between users.

3. Photos and Media

Users may upload profile photos, album photos, and chat images. Photos may be processed by automated safety and moderation services before being accepted or displayed.

Automated systems may be used to detect content that may violate VYRO Community Guidelines, including explicit sexual content, nudity, or prohibited drug-related imagery.

4. Messages and Communication

Messages and media exchanged through VYRO may be stored in order to provide chat functionality.

Users may also have access to features such as timed photos, private or locked albums, and album sharing.

5. Content Moderation and Safety

VYRO may use automated systems to detect prohibited content, suspicious behavior, spam, illegal drug solicitation, explicit imagery, or other violations of our rules.

Accounts may be warned, temporarily suspended, restricted, or banned when repeated or serious violations are detected.

Users may also report or block other users.

6. Who Viewed Me, Likes, and Matches

VYRO may record interactions between users, such as:

  • Profile views
  • Likes sent and received
  • Matches
  • Favorites
  • Blocked users

This information is used to provide social and discovery features inside the app.

7. Authentication

VYRO may use authentication services provided by third parties, including Supabase and Google Sign-In.

Authentication information is used to securely create and manage user accounts and login sessions.

8. Advertising

VYRO may use Google AdMob to display advertisements such as interstitial or rewarded ads.

Advertising providers may collect device identifiers, advertising data, and usage information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Third-Party Services

VYRO may use third-party services to provide app functionality, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and backend services
  • Google Sign-In for authentication
  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Image moderation services for automated content safety checks

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no online system can guarantee complete security.

11. Account Blocking and User Safety

Users may block other users. When a user is blocked, certain interactions such as messaging, profile discovery, or private album access may be limited or prevented.

12. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the app, comply with legal requirements, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

13. Children's Privacy

VYRO is intended only for users who are at least 18 years old.

We do not knowingly allow children under 18 to create accounts.

14. Your Choices

Depending on available app features, users may be able to:

  • Update their profile information
  • Change profile photos
  • Remove album photos
  • Block or unblock users
  • Control private album access
  • Delete or manage certain account data

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

16. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or VYRO, please contact the developer through the contact information provided in the app's official store listing.
