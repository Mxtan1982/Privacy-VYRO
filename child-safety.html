VYRO Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 1, 2026

VYRO is intended only for users aged 18 and above. VYRO has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following content and behavior are strictly prohibited on VYRO:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of minors
  • Grooming of minors for sexual purposes
  • Sextortion involving minors
  • Soliciting, requesting, sharing, distributing, or possessing sexual content involving minors
  • Trafficking, coercion, or exploitation of children
  • Any attempt to sexualize, endanger, exploit, or facilitate harm to a minor

2. Age Requirement

VYRO is an adults-only social application. Users must be at least 18 years old to create or use a VYRO account.

Accounts believed to belong to minors may be restricted, suspended, or removed where appropriate.

3. In-App Reporting

VYRO provides in-app reporting tools that allow users to report users, profiles, messages, or content that may violate our safety standards.

Reports involving suspected child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, sextortion, or other child safety risks are treated as serious safety matters.

4. Moderation and Enforcement

VYRO may use automated and manual safety measures to detect, review, and act on prohibited content or behavior.

Enforcement actions may include:

  • Removing prohibited content
  • Restricting or suspending accounts
  • Permanently terminating accounts
  • Blocking further access to VYRO services
  • Preserving relevant information when legally required

5. Reporting to Authorities

When VYRO becomes aware of suspected or confirmed CSAM or CSAE activity, VYRO will take appropriate action and comply with applicable child safety laws.

Where required by applicable law, VYRO may report relevant violations to appropriate regional, national, or other competent authorities and cooperate with lawful investigations.

6. Cooperation and Legal Compliance

VYRO is committed to complying with applicable laws and regulations related to child protection, online safety, abuse prevention, and the reporting of illegal child sexual abuse material.

7. User Responsibility

Users must not upload, request, distribute, encourage, or facilitate any content or conduct involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors.

Users who encounter suspected child safety violations should use VYRO's in-app reporting tools immediately.
