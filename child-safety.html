VYRO Child Safety Standards VYRO has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). VYRO is intended only for users aged 18 and above. The following content and behavior are strictly prohibited: - Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) - Sexual exploitation or abuse of minors - Grooming of minors for sexual purposes - Sextortion involving minors - Solicitation, sharing, requesting, or distribution of sexual content involving minors - Trafficking or exploitation of children - Any attempt to sexualize, endanger, or exploit a minor Users can report other users and inappropriate content directly within the VYRO app. When VYRO becomes aware of suspected CSAM or CSAE activity, appropriate action may include removing content, restricting or terminating accounts, preserving relevant information where legally required, and reporting confirmed violations to appropriate regional or national authorities as required by applicable law. VYRO complies with applicable child safety laws and cooperates with lawful investigations where required. Child Safety Contact: mmluckyteam@gmail.com
